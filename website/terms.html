What the service is

Responsibilities is at an early stage. The service is a remote MCP server that keeps a persistent record for AI agents: responsibilities and their versions, runs, the facts agents observe for each subject, cases, and the decisions agents make about them, so that the next run can start from the last. It checks what agents submit against the rules of the responsibility, for example that an assessment is one of the responsibility’s labels, and stores it.

The service does not read your systems, run agents, call AI models, schedule runs or take actions. The agents and MCP clients you connect do that, under their own terms. The service does not check whether recorded facts are true or decisions are right. The people who use agents remain accountable for what those agents record and do.

Access

Access is by invitation. We create workspaces and add members by email address, and may add or remove members and withdraw access at any time. You sign in with a Google account whose verified email address belongs to an active member. Each person can be an active member of one workspace at a time, and what is recorded in a workspace is shared with all of its members.

An MCP client you approve acts in your workspace on your behalf. Keep the credentials your clients hold to yourself, and tell us at info@responsibilities.dev if you think they have been misused.
